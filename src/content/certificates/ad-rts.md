## 🎓 Certified Active Directory Red Team Specialist (AD-RTS)

The **Certified Active Directory Red Team Specialist (AD-RTS)** certification by **Cyberwarfare Labs** is an advanced, hands-on red team program centered around enterprise Active Directory attack simulations. Conducted over **30 days**, the certification focuses on practical adversary emulation in realistic corporate environments rather than theoretical exercises. Participants are required to identify and exploit vulnerabilities across **Active Directory, Active Directory Certificate Services (ADCS), Microsoft Exchange, and ESXi** infrastructures while building complete attack chains—from reconnaissance and initial access to persistence, lateral movement, privilege escalation, and data exfiltration. The program emphasizes real-world offensive security techniques, operational tradecraft, and the methodologies used by modern red teams during enterprise engagements.
