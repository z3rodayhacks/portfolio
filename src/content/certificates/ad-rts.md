The **AD-RTS** certification by **Cyberwarfare Labs** is an advanced, hands-on red team program centered around enterprise Active Directory attack simulations. Conducted over 30 days, it focuses on practical adversary emulation in realistic corporate environments - identifying and exploiting vulnerabilities across Active Directory, ADCS, Microsoft Exchange, and ESXi infrastructures while building complete attack chains from reconnaissance through data exfiltration.
