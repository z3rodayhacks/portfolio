## 🏆 Thiran CTF 2026

**Thiran CTF 2026** was a multi-round Capture The Flag (CTF) competition organized by **Sri Eshwar College of Engineering** and **Team KNULL CTF**, bringing together students and cybersecurity enthusiasts to compete in a fast-paced offensive security environment. Participants tackled challenges across **Digital Forensics, Steganography, Cryptography, Web Security, Reverse Engineering, and OSINT**, requiring a combination of technical expertise, analytical thinking, and teamwork. The event served as an excellent platform to strengthen practical cybersecurity skills, explore real-world attack scenarios, and collaborate with some of the brightest security researchers in the community. Team **0xZ3roDay** secured **3rd Place**, continuing our consistent performance in competitive cybersecurity events.
